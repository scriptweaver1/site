**As this is a Warhammer 40k script, there are some weird words of the T’au alien species around here and there in this script, so this is a glossary for them.** 
**Don’t worry about pronunciation too much, these words don't have a right way to be spoken as the books are contracting on how it sounds in any case. Just use your best judgment and everything will turn out fine!**

**Glossary:**   

**Se'hen che lel = “riding the lightning” / traveling too fast**

**Ui't = A small, rodent like animal**

**Dec'taa = second**

**Fu'llassoo = A political mess, Troublesome**

**Gue'la = Human**

**Gue’vessa = Human who lives in T’au society**

**J'karra = Mirror**

**Ko'vash = a worthy cause**

**Hy-husa = Alcoholic beverage, drunk in a single gulp while still warm**

**Mont'au = individualistic t'au**

**Ta'lissera = marriage or bonding ritual**
